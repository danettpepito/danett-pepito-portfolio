Jumble


About

There's so much focus that sits on polished final outcomes, while process, experimentation, and failure often get left out, even though that’s usually where most of the thinking happens.

This led me to idea of Jumble, a web-based media platform for creatives to share the process behind their work through articles and reflections and challenge the perception of polished creative work by showing its messy process

Role UX/UI Design, Responsive Design
Timeline 8 weeks
Practice Academic, Individual Project
Tools Figma, VS Code, Illustrator

REFLECTION

I thought...

this project made me realise I’m more interested in understanding people than designing interfaces. I became more drawn to the thinking behind user behaviour. What people do, why they do it, and how those patterns can inform better design decisions. Instead of focusing purely on the final output, I found myself valuing the process of research, observation, and uncovering insight.
