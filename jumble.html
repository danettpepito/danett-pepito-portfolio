Jumble


About

During my creative practice, I noticed how much emphasis was placed on polished final outcomes. Process, experimentation, and failure were often hidden away, even though they were the most meaningful parts of making. At the same time, I found myself wanting to hear more honest stories from other creatives — not just what they made, but how they made it, what went wrong, and how their ideas evolved over time. I decided to explore this by designing and building Jumble, a web-based media platform where emerging creatives can document and share the messy realities of the creative process through articles, reflections.
