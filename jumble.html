Designing a space for process, not perfection

2025 | Web-based media company

Jumble


About

There's so much focus that sits on polished final outcomes, while process, experimentation, and failure often get left out, even though that’s usually where most of the thinking happens.

This led me to idea of Jumble, a web-based media platform for creatives to share the process behind their work through articles and reflections and challenge the perception of polished creative work by showing the process.

Role UX/UI Design, Responsive Design
Timeline 8 weeks
Practice Academic, Individual Project
Tools Figma, VS Code, Illustrator

Challenge

Create and pitch a web-based media company that addresses a need from young people in Aotearoa New Zealand.

Challenge

Creative work online is often consumed through polished final outcomes, with little visibility into the process behind it.

Solution

A media platform centred around process-driven stories, reflections, and the thinking behind creative work.


DISCOVER

I approached this project by asking:

How you feel after reading the content on the platform?

Why do you consume these platforms?

What are some problems you find with the current media industry?


And then I mapped my assumptions that I've noticed within young people New Zealand and did further academic research based on these. After an affinity map which lead me to:


Main insight from research

There is no intent when going on media, it’s just for instant gratification and mindless scrolling


Define

To understand the why behind the users behaviours, I conducted a couple of in depth interviews on:

1. Their behaviours and habits on media platforms


2. Problems in media industry


Creative Block

Consuming large amounts of creative content is counterproductive to finding inspiration

Formality

Intimidated by traditional media because it’s so wordy, too serious, lots of formal jargon


How might we...

Create a media platform that encourages creatives to share the messy realities of their creative process?

Challenge

  • Creative work is mostly shown through polished final outcomes
  • Process, experimentation, and failure are often hidden
  • Existing creative platforms prioritise finished work over creative thinking

Goals

  • Create a space focused on process-driven creative stories
  • Encourage transparency around creative experimentation
  • Make behind-the-scenes thinking easier to discover and engage with

OUTCOME

A coded responsive media platform with a focus on process and reflection, allowing creatives to share the messy realities of their creative journey. The platform challenges the perception of polished creative work by showcasing its messy process, fostering a more authentic and supportive creative community.


1. Discover

Users browse curated articles and discussions focused on creative process

2. Search

Users can search for specific topics, tags, or creators to find relevant content

3. Save

Users can bookmark articles to revisit later
