Sorta


About

I noticed that even when people want to dispose of food waste correctly, the process often sits in the background of everyday life. It’s not that there’s no intention. It’s that the systems around it don’t always make the “right” choice feel simple or automatic.

So I designed Sorta, a food sorting system paired with a companion app as part of a new enterprise concept. The aim was to explore how clearer information, small moments of reinforcement, and reduced decision fatigue could make food disposal feel more intuitive and less like an extra task people need to think about.
