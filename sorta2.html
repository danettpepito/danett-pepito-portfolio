Rethinking food waste through simple, daily action.

2025 | New enterprise project

Sorta


About

I noticed that even when people want to dispose of food waste correctly, it often isn’t simple or automatic in everyday life.

I designed Sorta, a food sorting system and companion app, to explore how clearer information and reduced decision fatigue can make food disposal more impactful to encourage its use.

Role UX Research, Service Design
Timeline 8 weeks
Practice Academic | Individual Project
Tools Figma, Illustrator, VSCode
Explore the process ↓


Challenge

I was tasked with creating and pitching a human-centred social or commercial enterprise in Auckland that uses design to solve a real-world problem and enhance social, cultural, or community well-being.

Problem

  • Food waste often happens in everyday routines without much thought
  • Sustainable intentions don’t always translate into consistent action

Solution

  • A system/app that encourages clearer everyday disposal decisions
  • Gentle prompts that support better habits over time

How might we...

Make food scrap bin benefits more visible in everyday life?


The refined direction became:

Challenge

  • People struggle to understand where food scraps actually go
  • Bin systems feel inconvenient and easy to ignore
  • Awareness of impact is low in everyday disposal moments

Solution

  • An AI powered bin that tracks and weighs the scraps
  • A companion app, to see updates of the bin in real time

CLARIFY

After, proposing the solution, and did extensive research on competitors with similar products.

An example of how the competitor, Kitro, uses the idea

To further understand where I can position this in the market, I did further case studies and SWOT analysis on competitors ( kitro , winnow solutions , lasso ) with similar products.

I saw an opportunity to simplify the concept for the target audience and applied key features to my solution.


The purpose and features for the service became clear; I was able to define key features and goals for the service that would set it apart in the market.
