Data Leak


About

How might we use data visualisation through an interactive installation?

Data Leak is an interactive installation and experience where users answer a questionnaire to create a personalised “fish” that is uploaded into a projected tank, visualising how personal data exists and moves alongside others in digital systems.

Role UI Design, Backend Developer
Timeline 8 weeks
Practice Academic, Group Project
Tools Figma, VS Code, p5.js
Team Loveday Tan, Mhayvine Capulong, Chance Tavita-Jackson, Beilei Yang

Challenge

Propose a design-led response that investigates a specific problem within the digital legacy space. Explore how design can make these systems more humane, accessible, ethical, or culturally responsive.

My Role

My role as one of the interactive designers was more focused on the backend of the project. I was responsible for creative coding and interaction development, as well as assisting with interface design.



DISCOVER

It all started with a grandma

An AI’d grandma. We came across an ad for an app that allowed people to upload information about a deceased loved one so AI could recreate and memorialise them. The idea of a digital version of someone living on indefinitely felt unsettling, and that discomfort stayed with us as we explored the project further.

We noticed AI becoming more present in everyday apps like Google, Facebook, and Instagram. Looking at tools/apps like 2wai, which reconstruct and simulate human-like interactions from personal data, we started exploring an underlying discomfort in how AI interprets and reshapes information.



DEFINE

We took a lot of inspiration from this case study, taking what we learned and opportunities we could use. The wonder from this installation shaped our direction. We wanted people to feel curious enough to engage, rather than overwhelmed by the topic.


But... why fish?

The choice to use the fish metaphor was made to attract attention without outright scaring or overwhelming our audience on the subject of online data, so instead we aim to bring in attention with a visually engaging aquatic theme, to make it easier to understand.




CLARIFY

The idea was an interactive installation where users answer a questionnaire to create and upload their personalised “fish” into a projected tank, representing personal data, where they swim alongside others to visualise how shared information moves.


Our other interactive designer, Mhayvine, worked on developing the experience and campaign/installation touchpoints.



CONCEPTUALISE

Visual Direction

Initially, we went with a pixel style for the fish, but felt that this direction felt too childish.

We explored other visual styles, running a vote and asking participants to compare options, and landed on generative/abstract. Participants said it felt futuristic, eye-catching, and more reflective of data as a dynamic, evolving system compared to more literal or pixel-based styles.



Prototyping

I experimented with creative code using p5.js to develop the look and behaviours of the fish when spawned in the tank. This is where I continued to explore the abstract, generative style and play around more with the customisation easier, to make each fish look different.
