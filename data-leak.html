Data Leak


About

Our personal data is constantly collected, shared, and stored without much awareness or control.

Data Leak is an interactive experience that responds to how AI processes personal information and makes that invisible flow of data more tangible. Through a guided, reflective interface, it surfaces what is usually hidden behind algorithms, helping people understand and reflect on their exposed data in a more personal and immediate way.

But... why fish?

The choice to use the fish metaphor was made to attract attention without outright scaring or overwhelming our audience on the subject of Online Data, so instead Data-Leak will aim to bring in attention with a visually engaging aquatic theme, as well as the metaphors of Data and Sea working well together.
