About

In today’s fast-paced digital world, communication is often reduced to quick messages that feel fleeting and impersonal.

Outbox is an interactive writing experience that reimagines letter writing in a contemporary digital format. It has casual and approachable, using playful prompts and a guided flow to make starting a letter feel effortless.
